# Further Resources & Next Steps

You have covered Kotlin from the basics through coroutines and a full Android
architecture. Here is where to go next.

## Practice

- [Kotlin Koans](https://play.kotlinlang.org/koans) — bite-sized exercises in the
  browser.
- [Advent of Code](https://adventofcode.com/) — solve puzzles in Kotlin.
- Extend the capstone projects with the challenges at the end of
  [Part 9]({% link part9/index.md %}).

## Deepen your Kotlin

- [Effective Kotlin](https://kt.academy/book/effectivekotlin) — idioms and best
  practices.
- [Kotlin Multiplatform](https://kotlinlang.org/docs/multiplatform.html) — share
  code across Android, iOS, web, and desktop.
- [Ktor](https://ktor.io/) — build backends in Kotlin.

## Deepen your Android

- [Now in Android](https://github.com/android/nowinandroid) — Google's reference
  app showcasing modern architecture and modularization.
- [Android Codelabs](https://developer.android.com/courses) — hands-on,
  step-by-step tutorials.
- [Compose Pathways](https://developer.android.com/courses/pathways/compose) —
  official Compose learning path.

## Stay current

- [Kotlin Blog](https://blog.jetbrains.com/kotlin/)
- [Android Developers Blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/)
- [Talking Kotlin podcast](https://talkingkotlin.com/)
